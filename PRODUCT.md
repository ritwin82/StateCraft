# Statecraft
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Students learning Theory of Computation and instructors reviewing class misconceptions.
## Product Purpose
Build DFA, NFA, and ε-NFA on a canvas, simulate strings, and learn from verified counterexamples and progressive hints.
## Capabilities and Constraints
The deterministic TypeScript engine is the sole correctness authority. Ollama only phrases verified facts. React, React Flow, Zustand, Tailwind, Express, Prisma/PostgreSQL, and Vitest are required by the brief. Local practice must remain useful without account setup.
## Brand Commitments
The user selected a warm light workspace with a charcoal navigation rail and one deep teal accent. Flat stone, parchment and mineral-grey surfaces, self-hosted Manrope and DM Sans, square sections and restrained control corners define the interface. All explicit visual restrictions are recorded in DESIGN.md and .impeccable/design.json.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive layout, keyboard-accessible graph and form editing, visible focus, and reduced-motion support.
